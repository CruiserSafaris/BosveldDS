About Bosveld Draughting Service

Local knowledge, creative thinking, straightforward service.

With 45 years of experience behind every set of plans, Bosveld Draughting Service is built on knowing this part of Limpopo — the soil, the municipal processes, the climate, and the way people actually build out here.

That local knowledge is paired with creative thinking on every project, so your plans fit your site and your budget, not just a template. Every client works directly with the person drawing their plans — honest, trustworthy service from someone who has been doing this a long time.
